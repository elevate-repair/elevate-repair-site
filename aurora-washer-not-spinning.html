Why Your Washer Is Not Spinning

When your washer in Aurora refuses to spin, it might be due to a variety of issues. One of the most common reasons is an unbalanced load. If your washer senses an imbalance, it may decide not to spin to avoid damaging the drum. Aurora residents might also experience this problem due to the type of water available, which can lead to detergent buildup affecting the washer’s performance.

Another frequent issue is a malfunctioning lid switch. This safety feature prevents the washer from spinning if the lid is open. In the humid climate of Aurora, this component can sometimes fail prematurely, causing the washer to remain idle. Power supply issues in homes, potentially affected by storms common to the area, can also lead to non-spinning washers if the circuit breaker is tripped.

Additionally, worn-out belts and motor couplings are mechanical components that often wear down over time. Given Aurora’s varying temperatures, materials can expand and contract, exacerbating wear and tear. Regular maintenance can help mitigate these issues, but if they persist, professional repair is recommended.

What Our Technicians Inspect

  • Lid Switch: Ensures the lid is closed properly and signals the washer to spin.

  • Clutch Assembly: Engages the washer’s spin basket, essential for proper spinning.
  • Control Board: Manages electrical functions, ensuring commands are followed accurately.
  • Drain Pump: Removes water efficiently to allow proper spinning action.

Common Questions About Washer Not Spinning Issues

Why is my washer not spinning but the motor is running?

This issue often points to a problem with the drive belt or motor coupling, both of which our technicians inspect during repairs. Aurora’s climate can sometimes affect these components, leading to wear over time.

What can I do if my washer won’t spin?

Before calling for repairs, check that the lid is closed properly and that the load is balanced. If these steps don’t resolve the issue, professional inspection is recommended, as the problem might be more complex.

Is there a quick fix for a washer that doesn’t spin?

In some cases, redistributing the load or resetting the circuit breaker can solve the problem. However, persistent issues should be evaluated by a professional to avoid further damage.

Washer Repair Considerations for Aurora Homes

Aurora’s large family households—particularly in the southeast communities near Smoky Hill Road and E-470—place heavy demand on residential washers. Front-load high-efficiency washers from builder packages in Aurora commonly develop drum bearing failures and worn suspension components around the 8- to 12-year mark. High-spin cycles accelerate bearing wear, and heavy load use in large households compounds the rate of failure.

Many Aurora townhome and condo units have stacked laundry configurations in closet-sized spaces. Suspension rod and shock absorber replacement on a stacked unit requires full clearance around the machine, and many Aurora alcove installations don’t provide it without pulling the unit out. Our technicians arrive prepared for limited-access work and can service stacked configurations efficiently in tight laundry closets.

Aurora’s water hardness causes scale buildup in detergent dispenser channels and on drum interior surfaces. Mineral and detergent residue can coat load sensors and drum walls, contributing to balance detection errors that prevent the spin cycle from engaging. A combination of descaling and mechanical inspection is often needed to fully resolve recurring spin failures in Aurora homes.
